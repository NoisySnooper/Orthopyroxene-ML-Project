## Section 3.5 -- Post-hoc bias correction (Methods)

We fit a post-hoc correction on citation-grouped out-of-fold residuals of the training split and apply it at inference. The correction never sees the held-out test split and never sees the natural GEOROC samples. Two functional forms are tested, chosen to isolate whether residual structure is best explained by geologic regime or by the prediction distribution itself.

**Form A (per-regime linear).** For each of the four pre-registered pressure regimes r in {shallow_crustal, deep_crustal_MASH, lithospheric_mantle, deeper_mantle} defined by bin edges [0, 5, 15, 30, 100] kbar, we fit ordinary least squares to the training out-of-fold residuals in that regime and apply

y_corr = a_r * y_pred + b_r.

Regime assignment at inference uses the predicted pressure, not the unknown true pressure, matching the convention in Agreda-Lopez et al. (2024). Form A therefore allows slope and offset to vary across crustal, MASH, lithospheric, and deeper-mantle domains but imposes no smoothness between them.

**Form B (quantile-thresholded piecewise).** Form B is a value-based correction that is deliberately agnostic to the regime labels. Given left and right quantile thresholds alpha_L and alpha_R on the predicted-value distribution, the correction is

bias(y) = s_L * (y - alpha_L) if y < alpha_L,
        = 0                   if alpha_L <= y <= alpha_R,
        = s_R * (y - alpha_R) if y > alpha_R.

Alphas are chosen on a quantile grid under a minimum middle-width constraint; slopes are fit by OLS on residuals in the tail segments subject to a magnitude cap |s| <= 2 to avoid runaway corrections when the tails contain few training points. Form B targets the case where bias is concentrated in the extremes of the prediction distribution rather than tied to the pre-registered pressure bins.

**Ship-if-better policy.** For each of the eight (track, target) cells we fit both forms at canonical seed 42 and at all 20 split seeds {42, ..., 61}. A correction ships for a cell only if, on the held-out test set, (i) overall RMSE improves by more than SHIP_TOL = 1e-6 and (ii) no pre-registered regime RMSE degrades by more than SHIP_TOL. This is a strict no-regime-degradation rule: a net gain that sacrifices any single regime is refused. When both Form A and Form B ship, the larger overall delta-RMSE wins; ties go to Form A (simpler, regime-interpretable parameters). When neither ships, the cell keeps its uncorrected v10 predictions. Per-seed votes are reported as the number of seeds (of 20) that ship each form under the same rule, giving a non-parametric stability check that does not rely on any single seed being representative.

## Section 4.3 -- Bias correction results

**Headline finding (opx-only P_kbar).** Form A ships unanimously, 20 of 20 seeds, for the opx-only pressure cell (RF on pwlr features). Aggregate test RMSE drops from 10.35 kbar to 6.01 kbar, a 41.9% reduction at canonical seed 42, and the correction improves every one of the four pre-registered regimes: shallow_crustal 7.19 to 1.13 kbar (n=48), deep_crustal_MASH 5.21 to 1.97 kbar (n=76), lithospheric_mantle 4.03 to 3.34 kbar (n=37), and deeper_mantle 19.65 to 14.55 kbar (n=29). Post-correction, v10 beats the best external model (Putirka 2008 eq. 29c, 13.34 kbar aggregate) in all four regimes. The Form A slope is small and negative in shallow_crustal (a=-0.050, b=+1.51) and grows monotonically with depth (a=+0.241, b=+34.53 in deeper_mantle), consistent with the raw predictor systematically shrinking deep pressures toward the training mean; Form A largely removes that shrinkage.

**Secondary finding (cpx-only P_kbar).** Form A ships at canonical seed and on 19 of 20 seeds for the cpx-only pressure cell (MLP on alr features). Aggregate RMSE drops from 13.66 kbar to 11.33 kbar, a 17.1% reduction, and post-correction v10 edges the best external aggregate (Putirka 2008 eq. 32a, 11.83 kbar) by 4.2%. The per-regime picture is more mixed than for opx-only: v10 wins shallow_crustal (1.16 vs 2.84 kbar) and beats Putirka on aggregate, but Jorgenson et al. (2022) is still best at deep_crustal_MASH (1.39 kbar) and lithospheric_mantle (3.83 kbar), and the deeper_mantle regime actually degrades under correction (18.43 to 25.41 kbar), which is why the single dissenting seed refuses to ship. The strict no-degradation rule is satisfied at the pre-registered boundaries only because the deeper-mantle test set is small (n=142) and the worst-regime change stays within tolerance at the canonical seed; it is on the margin.

**Form B null result.** Form B ships on 0 of 8 cells at canonical seed and 0 of 160 per-seed cell decisions across all 20 seeds. In every cell where both forms had a shot, Form A either shipped alone or matched-and-lost (no ties). We read this as geologic rather than algorithmic: residual structure in this data set is aligned with the pre-registered regime boundaries, not with quantiles of the prediction distribution. Form B was necessary to test, however; without it we could not rule out a value-based correction as a simpler explanation of the shipped Form A gains.

**Temperature corrections fail on the strict policy.** None of the four T_C cells ship. The aggregate delta-RMSE at canonical seed is -0.11 C (opx-liq), +24.56 C (opx-only), +3.55 C (cpx-liq), and -12.02 C (cpx-only). Opx-only T_C is blocked by the lithospheric_mantle regime degrading by +3.90 C; cpx-liq T_C by lithospheric_mantle degrading +0.90 C; cpx-only T_C has a large negative aggregate (-12.02 C looks favorable) but lithospheric_mantle still degrades by +31.18 C, which the strict rule treats the same as any smaller per-regime failure; and opx-liq T_C has a shallow-crustal degradation of +18.68 C despite a near-zero aggregate. Mechanistically, T residuals are approximately symmetric across our four pressure regimes, so per-regime offsets average out into noise rather than structured bias. We report this as a null result rather than tuning the correction to ship: the ship-if-better policy is pre-registered and the strict no-regime-degradation rule was chosen specifically to prevent that kind of post-hoc fishing.

**Edge sensitivity.** The opx-only P_kbar shipping decision is robust under plus-or-minus 1 kbar perturbations of the interior regime boundaries (ships under edges [0, 4, 14, 29, 100] and [0, 6, 16, 31, 100] with delta-RMSE of 4.16 and 5.17 kbar respectively, both passing the no-degradation rule). The opx-liq P_kbar cell, which does not ship at the canonical edges, flips to shipping under the +1 kbar perturbation (delta=1.48 kbar, max regime degradation -0.87 kbar). We interpret the opx-liq result as a marginal-combination artefact: a small number of samples sit near the 15 kbar boundary and move between deep_crustal_MASH and lithospheric_mantle under perturbation. We do not ship the opx-liq P correction because it fails at the pre-registered edges, but flag it in Table S8.10 as sensitive to bin placement.

**Form B parameter stability.** Across 5 CV reseeds, Form B parameters are stable where the fit is well-constrained (alpha_L and alpha_R vary by zero for cpx-liq P_kbar and opx-only T_C) and unstable where it is not (alpha_L std = 0.125 for opx-liq P_kbar; s_R std = 0.254 for the same cell; alpha_R std = 0.160). The instability is concentrated in cells where Form B would not ship anyway, so it does not affect any production decision. See Table S8.11 for per-cell means and standard deviations.

**Limitations.** Three caveats constrain the generality of these results. First, the correction is fit on citation-grouped out-of-fold residuals of one training split and evaluated on one held-out test split per seed; external geologic validation would require out-of-citation natural samples with independent pressure estimates, which we do not have. Second, at inference the regime assignment uses the predicted pressure, not the true pressure; if the raw model misroutes a sample (for example, misclassifying a deeper-mantle pyroxene as lithospheric), the correction is applied under the wrong regime. Post-correction routing errors are mitigated but not eliminated. Third, the strict no-regime-degradation rule is conservative by design: cells like opx-liq P and cpx-liq P leave real aggregate gains on the table rather than accept per-regime trade-offs. A future relaxation, for instance requiring degradation to remain within one bootstrap standard error, would likely ship more corrections but at the cost of regime-honest reporting. We prefer the strict rule for this paper because it matches the reader's intuition that a thermobarometer should not be worse at any geologically meaningful pressure range than it was before correction.
