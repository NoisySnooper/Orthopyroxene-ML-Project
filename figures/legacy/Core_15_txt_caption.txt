Figure 15. Feature-importance concordance across tuned families per (track, target) cell. Eight per-cell heatmaps show Spearman rank correlations of permutation importance between the eight tuned families (ElasticNet, RF, ERT, GB, XGB, LightGBM, CatBoost, MLP); TabPFN v2 is omitted because its kernel does not expose an estimator instance compatible with sklearn permutation_importance, documented as a protocol limitation. Permutation importance is computed on the held-out test set with 20 permutations per feature, seed 42; each feature's importance per family is ranked, and rows are intersected across families before computing the per-pair Spearman rho. Red-to-blue divergent colormap centered at rho = 0; a high Spearman rho indicates agreement on feature ordering even when coefficient magnitudes and feature sets differ across families. Per-cell median pairwise Spearman rho: opx_liq T = 0.58, opx_liq P = 0.59, opx_only T = 0.25, opx_only P = 0.37, cpx_liq T = 0.75, cpx_liq P = 0.36, cpx_only T = 0.51, cpx_only P = 0.42. High concordance on T-cells (cpx_liq T = 0.75) indicates that independent architectures identify the same thermodynamic drivers. The lower opx_only T rho (0.25) reflects the small-n intersection forced by feature-space heterogeneity across raw / alr / pwlr rather than genuine disagreement on petrology. Source: results/feature_concordance_permutation_importance.csv and results/feature_concordance_spearman_matrix.csv.
