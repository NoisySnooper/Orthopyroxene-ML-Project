Figure 17. Partial-dependence curves across tuned families, opx cells only. Four rows correspond to the four opx (track, target) cells (opx_liq T, opx_liq P, opx_only T, opx_only P); three columns correspond to the top-3 SHAP features of each cell's winner model. Each panel overlays the partial-dependence curve from eight tuned families (ElasticNet, RF, ERT, GB, XGB, LightGBM, CatBoost, MLP; TabPFN omitted as in Core_15) evaluated on a 30-point grid spanning the 2nd-to-98th percentile of the feature distribution on the training set. Families are drawn in the canonical MODEL_COLORS palette with the winner family emphasized. A thin gray histogram at the bottom of each panel shows the training-set density of the feature. All 12 cell-by-feature panels show consistent monotonic direction across families, with crossovers confined to the tail 10% of the feature distribution (where training density is sparse). This agreement rules out "families win by learning opposite mechanisms" as an explanation for cell-by-cell winner changes: architectures disagree on magnitude at the edges but agree on the direction of the physicochemical effect. Source: results/partial_dependence_across_families.parquet (2160 rows).
