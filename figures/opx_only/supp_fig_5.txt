Supp. Figure 5. Feature concordance heatmaps for the four opx (track, target) cells. Each cell shows the pairwise Spearman ρ across the 8 tuned families on permutation-importance ranks (20 repeats, seed 42). TabPFN is omitted from the matrix (no fitted sklearn estimator exposed to permutation_importance). Source: results/feature_concordance_spearman_matrix.csv.
