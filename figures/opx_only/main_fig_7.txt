Figure 7. Per-regime scorecard delta between our post-bias-correction RMSE and the best-available Putirka 2008 equation for the four opx (track, target) cells. Color encodes fractional improvement (Putirka RMSE − post-correction RMSE) / Putirka RMSE on a diverging red–white–green scale: green = ML wins, red = Putirka wins. External references are Putirka 2008 thermobarometers run through Thermobar: opx-liq T = eq 28a, opx-liq P = eq 29a (29b in deeper_mantle), opx-only P = eq 29c.
