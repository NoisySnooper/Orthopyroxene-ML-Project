Figure 14. Surrogate decision trees fit to each per-cell winning model. A depth-3 tree is trained to predict the winning model output as a function of input features on the OOF training predictions; reported R^2 quantifies how well a small tree approximates the deeper learner. A high surrogate R^2 means the underlying model is dominated by a few axis-aligned splits; a low surrogate R^2 means the model uses richer structure (interactions, smoothness) that a tree cannot recover.
