Figure 12. Classical-equivalence regression. For each of the four opx (track, target) cells, columns: (1) ML predicted versus classical-fit predicted in the target unit, with the 1:1 line and the per-cell tolerance band; (2) the classical-fit residuals; (3) the ML-minus-classical-fit disequilibrium map showing where the ML diverges from a closed-form classical functional form. High R^2 in column 1 means the ML prediction is approximately a classical formula plus a smooth residual; low R^2 means the ML is doing something a classical equation cannot capture.
