Figure 1. ExPetDB experimental coverage for the two opx pipelines, colored by pre-registered pressure regime. Panel (a) opx + liquid (n = 600, 93 citations); panel (b) opx only (n = 1035, 123 citations). Pre-registered regime edges are shallow_crustal <5 kbar, deep_crustal_MASH 5–15 kbar, lithospheric_mantle 15–30 kbar, deeper_mantle ≥30 kbar (locked 2026-04-17 before any correction fitting). Dashed horizontal lines mark the regime boundaries. Marker opacity distinguishes the 80/20 citation-grouped train/test split: train points faded, test points with dark edges. Marginal histograms show univariate T and P coverage. The summary block below each scatter reports n_total, n_train, n_test, citation count, 1st–99th percentile ranges, and per-regime counts.
