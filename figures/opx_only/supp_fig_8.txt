Supp. Figure 8. Surrogate decision trees for the four opx (track, target) cells. A DecisionTreeRegressor(max_depth=4, min_samples_leaf=10, seed 42) is fit to each per-cell winner's ML predictions on raw features. Each page shows one cell; reported R² is the surrogate fidelity with 500-bootstrap 95% CI. A high R² (≥0.85) means the ML is well-approximated by a depth-4 flowchart; a low R² means the ML uses smooth/interaction structure the tree cannot recover. Source: results/surrogate_tree_r_squared.csv.
